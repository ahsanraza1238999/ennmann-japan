Our Story

A Quiet Corner of Japan
in the Heart of Budapest

Ennmann opened its doors in 2008 with a simple belief — that authentic Japanese cuisine belongs not in grand dining halls, but in intimate rooms where every grain of rice matters. Named for the warmth of Japanese hospitality, our restaurant has become a beloved destination for those who understand that great sushi is a craft, not a commodity.

Our chef brings decades of training to every plate — from the precise temperature of the sushi rice to the angle of each cut. The fish is sourced with obsessive care, the wasabi is real, and the soy sauce is brewed. Nothing is rushed. Nothing is shortcut. This is Japanese cooking as it should be.

"The knife does the talking." — The Ennmann philosophy

Sake & Tea

The Ceremony
of Drinking

In Japan, what you drink matters as much as what you eat. Our sake and tea selection is curated with the same care as our kitchen.

01
Premium Junmai Sake
Pure rice sake served chilled or warm — selected from small breweries in Niigata and Kyoto.
02
Matcha Ceremony
Stone-ground ceremonial grade matcha, whisked tableside — a quiet ritual to end your meal.
03
Sencha & Genmaicha
Japanese green teas sourced from Shizuoka — sencha for clarity, genmaicha for its roasted warmth.
04
Umeshu & Yuzu Liqueur
Japanese plum wine and citrus liqueur — served on the rocks or with soda, a gentle finish to any evening.
